## My Favorite Series

My favorite series is **Girl from Nowhere**.

- *Genre:* Thriller, Mystery, Dark Comedy
- *Country:* Thailand
- *Original Thai title:* Dek Mai (meaning "New Kid")
- *Seasons:* 2 (2018 and 2021)
- *Main character:* Nanno, played by Chicha Amatayakul
- *Director:* Nottapon Boonprakob
- *Where to watch:* Netflix

### About
*Girl from Nowhere* is a Thai thriller series that follows **Nanno**, a mysterious girl who keeps transferring to new schools. Wherever she goes, she uncovers the secrets and wrongdoings of the students and teachers around her.

Each episode tells a **different story**, so you can watch the episodes in almost any order. Nanno never gets hurt or loses. Instead, she makes people face the consequences of their own actions.

The series deals with serious real-life issues like:
- Cheating
- Bullying
- Corruption
- Social pressure

### Why I Like It
I like this series because of its **unexpected plot twists** and because every episode gives a different lesson.
